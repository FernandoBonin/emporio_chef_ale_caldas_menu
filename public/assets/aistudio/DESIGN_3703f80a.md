## Brand & Style

This design system defines the digital hospitality experience for an artisanal, authorial gastrobar rooted in Tatuapé, São Paulo. The identity marries the nocturnal intimacy of a low-lit cocktail lounge with the earthy rigor of high-craft kitchen technique. It serves epicureans, culinary explorers, and neighborhood connoisseurs seeking deliberate indulgence over mass dining.

The visual style blends **Atmospheric Luxury** with **Tactile Editorial Craft**. It relies on deep charred-wood carbon surfaces, metallic copper filigree, and warm parchment typography. The ambiance feels sensory, smoky, and intentional—reminiscent of bespoke cocktail ice, cast-iron searing, and handcrafted ceramics under amber pin-spot lighting.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is anchored in charcoal depth and illuminated by brushed metallic warm tones:

- **Primary (`#D4A373`)**: Warm brushed brass used for authoritative action items, active navigational elements, and primary pricing signifiers.
- **Secondary (`#C68B59`)**: Burnished copper applied to secondary interactions, section divider lines, and tags highlighting culinary craft techniques.
- **Tertiary (`#E6C280`)**: Pale gold shimmer reserved for badges, seasonal chef highlights, sommelier pairings, and interactive hover states.
- **Neutral (`#121110`)**: Smoked obsidian canvas tone that absorbs harsh ambient light, paired with an elevated surface tier of `#1A1816` and border strokes of `#2A2623`.
- **Text & Foreground**: Rich bone parchment (`#F7F4EE`) for dominant headlines and dish titles, transitioning to muted slate flax (`#D9D2C7` and `#A8A196`) for sensory descriptions and regulatory notations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual-engine architecture:

1. **Editorial Expression (Playfair Display)**: Delivers an authorial, Michelin-grade presence for menu category titling, tasting course labels, and signature storytelling. Characterized by transitional contrast, high-end serifs, and organic italic flourishes.
2. **Functional Clarity (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures rapid readability in low-light dining conditions for ingredient lists, allergen warnings, table booking pickers, and currency values.

All uppercase labels carry wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) to mirror premium printed tasting cards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an asymmetrical, editorial fluid-grid foundation designed to evoke open-canvas menu folios rather than mechanical dashboard slots:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers are capped at 1280px to preserve intimate line lengths. Tasting menus flow through staggered 7/5 column splits to balance imagery against description.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Menu items shift to single-column card flows with persistent bottom-anchored actions for digital table ordering.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through nocturnal tonal layering and diffused bronze illumination rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas (`#121110`)**: The deepest resting foundation.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#1A1816`)**: Menu categories, card containers, and dialog shells. Delineated by low-contrast hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.15)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#23201D`)**: Floating action panels, sticky order summaries, and hover states.
- **Atmospheric Glow**: Modal overlays and hero focal points use an ambient back-projected radial glow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphic Scrims**: Sticky navigation headers and wine list filters apply a `12px` blur paired with an 80% opacity carbon background (`rgba(18, 17, 16, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs subtle, architectural corner treatments (Level 1 / Soft). Edges mimic hand-trimmed heavyweight stationery and honed kitchen basalt:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Soft `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, preserving a crisp, tailored architectural presence.
- **Card Containers & Sheets**: `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px) to soften viewport boundaries without feeling playful.
- **Media Containers**: Food and beverage photography retains crisp, squared or minimally rounded framing to emphasize culinary plating geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations, Order Submission)**: Solid metallic fill (`#D4A373`) with contrast charcoal typography (`#121110`), bold weight, `4px` corner radius, and subtle amber glow upon `:hover`.
- **Secondary Action (Wine Pairing, Dish Details)**: Ghost button with a `1px` stroke in `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.35)`, rich cream text, transitioning to a full copper stroke on focus.
- **Tertiary / Inline**: Underlined bone text with an animated copper stroke that expands from center outwards.

### Food & Cocktail Cards
- Constructed with surface `#1A1816` encased in a hairline `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.12)` border.
- Includes asymmetric photography crops, authorial dish name in Playfair Display, highlighted ingredients in muted flax, and price callouts rendered in pale gold.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact indicators (e.g., "Assinatura do Chef", "Orgânico", "Harmonizado") using 10px uppercase tracking, surrounded by a 1px border of `#C68B59` with a 15% alpha background tint.

### Form Inputs & Reservation Fields
- Dark recessed background (`#141312`) with a bottom-only or subtle all-around hairline border. Text cursor and focus ring shine in amber (`#E6C280`). Labels rest in crisp uppercase sans-serif.

### Selection Controls
- Checkboxes and radio toggles feature custom diamond or soft-square silhouettes with brass center marks when activated.

### Gastronomic Pairings Drawer
- Slide-over contextual tray revealing sommelier notes, terroir origin, and suggested cocktail pairings, styled with frosted translucent charcoal and gold filigree dividers.
